About Seiryu

Seiryu is the home base for every club, crew, and side project we run — built so we don't have to cram forums, groupchats, and club news into a Discord sidebar. Instead of scrolling for context every time you open the server, everything gets a proper place: a forum thread you can actually find again, a groupchat that belongs to your club and nobody else's, and a news page for what each club's been up to.

Forums are for posts that should stick around — questions, announcements, long-form discussion. Clubs are the actual groups you're part of, each with its own page and roster. Groupchats are where a club actually talks day to day, live and unfiltered. Club news sits above the groupchat as the highlight reel — what happened, without needing to scroll back through chat to find it.
